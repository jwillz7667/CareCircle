# Code of Conduct

CareCircle is built for families navigating one of the hardest parts of life — keeping someone they love safe at home. The people who contribute to this project carry that responsibility into how they treat each other.

## Our standards

**Expected behavior**

- Be respectful. Disagreement is welcome; condescension and personal attacks are not.
- Assume good intent. Ask before assigning blame.
- Be precise. State what you observed and what you want changed.
- Credit others. Acknowledge prior work and ideas.
- Make space. Give junior contributors room to learn, and senior contributors room to be wrong.
- Protect privacy. Treat user data, health information, and unpublished company information as confidential by default.

**Unacceptable behavior**

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination based on race, color, ancestry, national origin, religion, age, disability, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, marital or family status, veteran status, socio-economic status, or any other protected class.
- Sexualized language or imagery; unwelcome advances; sustained disruption of discussions.
- Doxxing or sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Publishing confidential project information, customer data, or unredacted health information.
- Retaliating against someone for reporting a violation in good faith.

## Scope

This Code applies to all project spaces — the repository, code reviews, issue trackers, internal Slack/Discord channels, video calls, in-person events, conference talks, and anywhere a participant is representing CareCircle or Viral Venture LLC.

It also applies to private communication when that communication is connected to project participation (for example, a one-on-one DM about a PR).

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, contact `conduct@viral-ventures-llc.com`. Reports go to a small, designated group at Viral Venture LLC. We will:

1. Acknowledge receipt within 2 business days.
2. Review the report. Where appropriate, we will speak with the reporter, the named party, and any witnesses.
3. Decide on a response and communicate it to the reporter and, if applicable, the named party.
4. Keep the report confidential. We will only share details with people who need them to investigate or remediate, and only with the reporter's consent where practical.

Reports may be submitted anonymously, but anonymous reports limit our ability to follow up.

## Enforcement

Possible responses include, but are not limited to:

- A private conversation and informal warning.
- A formal written warning.
- A temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
- Removal of contributor access.
- For employees and contractors, additional employment-related action under the relevant agreement.

The maintainers retain discretion to choose the response proportionate to the violation. Repeated or severe violations will escalate.

## Conflicts of interest

If a report involves a maintainer, that maintainer will recuse themselves from the review and response.
