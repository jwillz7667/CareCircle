# Support

CareCircle is proprietary software developed by Viral Venture LLC.

## How to get help

| You are | Use |
|---|---|
| An end user of the iOS app | In-app **Settings → Help & Support**, or email `support@viral-ventures-llc.com` |
| An enterprise / clinical / agency customer | Your designated success contact at Viral Venture LLC, or `partnerships@viral-ventures-llc.com` |
| Investigating a potential vulnerability | Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Email `security@viral-ventures-llc.com`. Do **not** open a public issue. |
| A contributor with a process question | The [CONTRIBUTING guide](CONTRIBUTING.md); for anything not covered, email `support@viral-ventures-llc.com` |
| A press / media contact | `press@viral-ventures-llc.com` |
| A licensing inquiry | `licensing@viral-ventures-llc.com` |

## What we cannot help with

- We are not your healthcare provider. Always consult a qualified clinician for medical questions.
- We cannot recover an account that has lost both its Sign-in-with-Apple identity and its iCloud Keychain — this is an Apple-side identity, not ours.
- We do not provide implementation or integration help for third-party systems unless a separate professional-services agreement is in place.

## Service status
